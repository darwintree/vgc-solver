Date: 2026-09-08
Source: Root request for local terminal certificates in busted Disguise successors
Language: English

## Entries

### 1. Use conservative joint survival conditions before finer HP tails

Type: unresolved-implementation-decision

Context:
The prepared probability envelope rejects an intact Disguise root once, preventing later otherwise eligible nodes from obtaining certificates. Life Orb modifies raw damage and can exhaust the attacking Pokemon after a target KO. Contact also enables Rough Skin retaliation. A raw target-KO tail alone is insufficient to certify a terminal win.

Decision:
Separate solve-scoped native audit from per-node state admission. Admit exact native Disguise handlers only outside their complete native intact-species predicate, while retaining the no-transformation requirement. Admit Life Orb only through its native event-slot identities; native damage enumeration executes its modifier. Exclude suppressed items and enlarge the overflow bound for the modifier. Allow contact only when the target lacks the admitted Rough Skin retaliation handler.

For a first attacker with recoil, retain its KO lower mass only when initial HP exceeds ceil(baseMaxhp/10). For a responding attacker with recoil, retain response KO lower mass only when its initial lower HP minus the first move's maximum damage times maximum hits still exceeds that recoil bound. All excluded mass remains unknown. This sufficient condition preserves the joint damage/survival requirement without assuming independence between two probability tails.

Reason:
The bound is conservative relative to native truncation and excludes simultaneous exhaustion. Disguise callbacks are inert only after their native predicate is false; no case names or benchmark species configurations enter the certificate. Existing complete probability enumeration, accuracy, critical branches, secondary exclusion, HP regimes and per-node sharing remain intact.

Follow-up:
Native oracle checks cover recoil boundaries, first and responding holders, intact/busted nodes, accuracy/critical/secondary branches, transformed and suppressed states, contact retaliation and custom callback fallback. Measure node-preparation overhead across other fixtures before any adoption. No CPU used during initial source preparation.

Validation:
Typecheck/build pass. Initial terminal suite passed37 tests; two combined Play Rough response fixtures exceeded the existing50,000-branch independent native oracle limit, without a certificate containment failure. Separating response survival/crit checks (Shadow Claw) from first-hit accuracy/secondary checks (Play Rough) retained native rule coverage without raising the branch limit. The focused final11 tests all pass, including both formerly oversized survival fixtures. No production fix was needed.
